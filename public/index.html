Rand Pierce is a graduate of New York University's Tisch School of the Arts (Film Production).

For most of his career, he was a Graphic Artist and User Interface Designer for video games (Paul Rand Pierce). He worked for 13 years at LucasArts working on Star Wars and a variety of other video game and development projects.

Other employers and clients following that included Crowdstar, Glu Mobile, ngmoco, Akili Interactive, Twin Suns Corp and 2K Games.

He was the sole winner of Walt Disney Imagineering R&D's Living Worlds Transmedia story competition, and was employed as a narrative consultant for 1.5 years on the development of his own project.

POP POP is his first screenplay. A slate of other original screenplays is in development.
